# Fizz

[now-on-havoc]: https://havoc.app/package/fizz

[<img width="150" src="https://docs.havoc.app/img/badges/get_square.svg" />][now-on-havoc]

**Spread two fingers. Watch the badges go.**

Your home screen has been nagging you all day. Instead of opening every app just to clear a red dot, put two fingers on the screen and spread them — your icons ripple outward, the badges swell and fade, and half a second later the screen is quiet again.

No buttons, no menus, no hunting through settings. Just a gesture that already feels natural on a screen you pinch and spread every day.

**Stays quiet:** Turn on *Keep badges cleared* and Fizz keeps sweeping the counts away as they come back, so a single new notification doesn't undo your clean home screen. If you would rather have no feedback at all, haptics switch off in the same place — and so does the whole tweak.

**Feels like it belongs**: The ripple follows your fingers and settles with a small overshoot, and one soft impact closes the gesture, so it reads as something the system always did.

## Good to know

- iOS 15 or later, on a jailbroken device.
- Works on both roothide and rootless setups.
- One gesture clears everything; there is nothing to select first.
